Gui-Ping (Roy) Liang Master's student, Graduate Institute of National Development, NTU

Master's student at NTU GIND with a background in business and finance. His research utilizes Language Models to measure corporate political rhetoric, specializing in automated text analysis, quantitative methods, and GIS spatial analytics.

National Taiwan University

If you are a master’s or PhD student at the Graduate Institute of National Development, National Taiwan University, and you are looking for a thesis supervisor or a committee member, please get in touch. I also welcome enquiries from graduate students in other departments of the College of Social Sciences who would like me to serve on their thesis committee. I am especially glad to supervise or examine work on:

  • Electoral systems, legislative behaviour, and political representation
  • Indigenous public opinion, Indigenous political representation, and the behaviour of elected representatives
  • Large language models and AI systems as political and measurement actors, and text-as-data applications in social science research more broadly
  • Political misperception and belief updating, including survey experimental design
